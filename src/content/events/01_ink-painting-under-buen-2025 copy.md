Artist Johannes Agnar introduces participants to working with ink through a series of simple mark-making exercises. Using brushes, nibs, and diluted ink, the workshop explores gesture, texture, and the relationship between control and chance. Participants experiment with different tools and surfaces while developing their own visual language through quick studies and improvisation.
