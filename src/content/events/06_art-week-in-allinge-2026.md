For one week, we’re bringing together local artists, neighbors, visitors & friends to celebrate creativity, community, and the joy of making things together at Den Gamle Brandstation i Allinge , a pop-up wine bar on the coast of Bornholm.

Most events are free and open to everyone, including our live concert. If you’d like to dive a little deeper, you can also join:

- Sip & Paint — 75 DKK (wine sold separately)
- Wine Club — 450 DKK for one day, or 1200 DKK for the full week
- Community Dinner — 250 DKK

Our Communal Canvas will be taking place throughout the week, all materials will be provided for your creativity:)

Whether you’re here to paint, listen, taste, meet new people, or simply spend time together, there’s a place for you.

Come celebrate local art, love, and community with us in Allinge.
