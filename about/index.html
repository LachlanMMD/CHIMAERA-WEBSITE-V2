about

CHIMAERA is a Copenhagen collective built around one relationship: an artist who brings a practice, and someone meeting it for the first time.

who we are

CHIMAERA is a Copenhagen creative collective — a kreativt fællesskab built around art, design and tactile process.

what we do

We gather artists and the people who meet them halfway. Some of us bring a practice into the room — a craft, a way of working, something we've spent time on. Some of us are trying it for the first time. Every gathering is built around that exchange.

why we exist

Because making something is better than only consuming it. CHIMAERA exists for hands-on process over passive consumption — workshops and gatherings where people leave having made something themselves.
